Delivery,
the Dodovard way.

Tuesday — Saturday

We operate primarily on a pre-order basis. Same-day orders placed before 10:00 AM are treated as pre-orders.

  • Standard Lagos delivery: ₦2,950
  • Orchid, Ikorodu & surrounding areas: ₦4,000
  • Ajah, Festac & surrounding areas: ₦4,500

A little note

Same-day orders after 10:00 AM are express and attract a different delivery fee. All sales are final.

Our meals are best enjoyed warm. Reheat in a microwave for at least 2 minutes before serving.
