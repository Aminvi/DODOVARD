The story of Dodovard

A little dodo. A very big dream.

Behind Dodovard is a story of faith, imagination, persistence and a love for turning something familiar into something unforgettable.

The woman behind the idea

Dodovard was created by Amy Mordi, a lawyer, in 2026. The name came from a playful idea: “dodo wey go Harvard.” It is cheeky, ambitious and unmistakably Nigerian — exactly the spirit Amy wanted the brand to carry.

The idea first came to her during prayers. What started as an idea that could have stayed in her head became a dream she decided to chase. Step by step, Amy kept working on it, shaping the concept, imagining the food, and building Dodovard into a place where plantain gets to be the main character.

For us, plantain is more than a side dish. It is comfort, memory, celebration and home. Dodovard takes that familiar feeling and gives it a fresh personality — from stuffed plantain boats and smash plantain burgers to pancakes, sauces and everything in between.
